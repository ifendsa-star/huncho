Before we start

You'll scan your face from the front and both sides with your camera, so we can map it and suggest cuts that suit you. Here's what happens to your photos.

Stays on your device

The camera only turns on when you press Turn on camera, and the live video never leaves your device. Face mapping and the 3D model run in your browser. Nothing is saved.

Sent once for analysis

When you press Analyse, your photos and face measurements are sent to Claude, an AI by Anthropic, to estimate your face shape. They aren't kept by Huncho.

Gone when you leave

Close the page or press Delete my photos and everything is cleared. Nothing is shared with your barber unless you send it.
